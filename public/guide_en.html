  • 1 On first launch a client called "Me" is already created — your personal log for your own knives
  • 2 Tap "+ Client" at the top right to add your first client
  • 3 Tap the "+" (Sharpen) button in the centre of the bottom bar — a two-step form will open
  • 4 Step 1 — Intake: choose the client, enter the knife, steel and job type (sharpening / edge touch-up / repair). Tap "Sharpen now" to go straight to step 2, or "Save as accepted" to queue the knife and return to step 2 later
  • 5 Step 2 — Sharpening: set the angle with a slider (10–45° scale, 0.1° step), optionally enable a microbevel (defaults to 2° blunter than the main angle), then add stones and a price. Before/after photos — optional. Tap "Done" to close the sharpening, or "Accepted" to save and return later
  • 6 If you saved a knife as accepted — open the record and tap "SHARPEN": step 2 will open with the data already filled in
  • 7 In a finished record tap "Share photo" → "Photo report" — the app will draw a card with the knife, steel and stones that you can save or send to the client

🎙️ Voice input — log a sharpening hands-free beta
  • 1 Enable the beta: 💾 icon → "About" → toggle "Voice input". Requires internet; works on Android (unavailable on iPhone)
  • 2 Open the sharpening form and tap "Dictate" in the header — the app listens continuously, the last recognised phrase is shown at the bottom
  • 3 Each command starts with the field name. Intake: "client Ivan", "knife Victorinox", "steel D2", "job sharpening", "note very dull", then "next"
  • 4 Sharpening: "angle 20", "stone naniwa 1000" → "add", "price 500". Finish: "save" (knife accepted) or "done" (sharpening closed)
  • 5 Correct on the fly: "clear steel", "remove last stone", "repeat" (what was heard). Stop dictation — "stop"
⚡ You can also dictate a single field: tap the microphone next to it. Double-tap a suggestion below a field for quick selection from the stones, steels and knives list.

⚠️ Data is stored on your phone!
If you clear the browser, reset or lose your phone — your data is gone. Connect Yandex.Disk for automatic cloud backup, or make a file backup once a week.
3 Backup — how to save your data
🔄 Auto-backup is already on: every time you open the app it saves a copy of your data internally. But this copy lives on the same phone — if you clear the browser or lose your phone, it's gone too. Connect Yandex.Disk or make a file backup below.
☁️ Yandex.Disk (recommended)
  • 1 Open "Backup" (💾 icon) → "Yandex.Disk" section → tap "Connect Yandex.Disk"
  • 2 Sign in with your Yandex ID and grant access to the app folder
  • 3 Turn on "Auto-backup to cloud" — the app will upload a backup to Yandex.Disk every time it opens. The last 7 snapshots are kept.
To restore: "Backup" → "Yandex.Disk" → pick a snapshot → "Merge". Data on your phone won't be lost — only new records from the cloud are added. To fully replace your data — tap "Download file" and restore via "Choose file…".
File backup
  • 1 On the Clients screen tap the 💾 icon at the top right — the "Backup" screen will open
  • 2 Tap "Save backup (JSON)"
  • 3 The file apptochite-2026-05-22.json will be saved to the Downloads folder on your phone
  • 4 Or tap "Share backup…" — the system share sheet opens; send the file to yourself in Telegram (Saved Messages), email or cloud. The file arrives with a .txt extension — that's expected; it's accepted when you restore.
Restoring from backup
  • 1 Open "Backup" (💾 icon) → "Choose file…" → find your .json file.
    If the file is lost but the app is intact — tap "Restore from auto-backup"
  • 2 Check the preview (how many clients and sharpenings) and choose a mode:
    "Merge" — adds new records from the file and updates those that are newer in the file; data on your phone is not deleted. For using two devices or adding without data loss.
    "Replace all" — deletes current data and fully restores from the file. For transferring to a new phone or after a reset.
🔒 Privacy
  • ● Client data (names, phone numbers, Telegram handles, photos) and all sharpenings are stored only on your phone. No cloud, no registration, no password — the app works offline
4 Updates
  • 1 The app updates automatically — just open it while online. Nothing else needed
  • 2 You can check the version manually in "About" (💾 icon → "About") → "Check"
  • 3 If the developer asks you to reinstall — perform a full reset (see below)
5 Full reinstall
Make a file backup first! After clearing, data cannot be recovered.
  • 1 Open the cleaner page in Chrome:
    fiscalagent.github.io/AppTochite/cleaner.html
  • 2 Tap "Clear all" — all app data will be deleted
  • 3 Long-press the AppTochite icon on your home screen → choose "Uninstall"
  • 4 Reinstall the app (see section "Installing" on page 1)
  • 5 Open "Backup" → "Choose file…" → restore your data from the .json file
★ Useful features
  • 🔍Search — available on the Clients, History and Reference screens. Searches by name, phone, Telegram handle, knife or stone name.
  • 🏷️Filters — in History you can show only accepted or only done sharpenings; in the client card you can filter by a specific knife.
  • 🔁Repeat sharpening — in a finished record the "Repeat sharpening" button creates a new one with the same knife, steel and stones. Handy for regulars.
  • 🗜️Photo compression — if storage fills up, enable Settings → "Compress new photos" (JPEG, up to 1280 px — images 3–5× smaller).
  • 📊Export to Excel — Backup → "Download CSV" exports all sharpenings as a spreadsheet for Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.
  • 🪨Import your stones from a file — Reference → Stones → "Import CSV". You need a "Name" column and one grit scale (µm / FEPA / JIS / GOST) — the rest are calculated. UTF-8 or Win-1251 encoding.
  • 🔄Clean up steels and knives — Reference → Steels/Knives → "Export CSV", edit the file in Excel (remove what you don't need, add your own), then upload it back via "Sync": anything missing from the file gets deleted from the catalog — handy for trimming the built-in list of steel grades or knife models down to what you actually use.
  • 💳Digital business card — the "Me" client's card gets a "Business card" button: photo, company, specialization, services and contacts turn into a ready-made image with a QR code. Scanning it saves the contact instantly; the card itself can be shared to any messenger in one tap.
  • 🗑️Trash — accidentally deleted a client or sharpening? About → "Trash": deleted records are kept for 3 days and can be restored. Deleting a client also puts all their sharpenings in the trash — they come back together.
  • 🎮Trainers for sharpeners — in Reference the 🎮 icon opens "Games": "Arrange the stones" (order the stones by grit) and "True angle" (read the sharpening angle by eye). They train your eye and your memory for grit.
  • 🔢Sharpening counter — the header of the History screen shows how many knives you've sharpened in total.
  • 📤Share the app — at the bottom of the Clients screen the "Share the app" button sends friends and clients a link to the app via any messenger.
  • ❤️Support the project — the app is free. If you'd like to say thank you, there's a card number in "About" — tap it to copy.
